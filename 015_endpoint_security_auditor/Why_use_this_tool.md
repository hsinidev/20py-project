# Why Use the Endpoint Security Policy Auditor?

Security breaches often occur not because of zero-day exploits, but due to **misconfigured systems**. This tool bridges the gap between complex security benchmarks and actionable administration.

### 1. Zero UI Lag
Traditional security scanners freeze the system while working. Our **Asyncio-powered** engine performs checks in the background, keeping the dashboard smooth and responsive.

### 2. Industry Standard Alignment
Don't guess what's secure. Our policies are derived from **CIS Benchmarks**, the global standard for securing IT systems and data.

### 3. Actionable Intelligence
Most scanners only tell you what's wrong. We give you the **Remediation Script** immediately. This reduces the "Time-to-Fix" (TTF) from hours of research to seconds of execution.

### 4. Modern Enterprise Aesthetic
The **Clinical-Blue** design offers a premium, professional experience. It's built to be used by both system administrators and security auditors who value clarity and data visualization.

### 5. Local & Private
Audit results stay on your machine. We use a local SQLite database, ensuring your system vulnerabilities are never uploaded to the cloud.
