# Why Use This Tool?

## 🎯 Purpose & Reason
The **Automated Phishing Campaign Simulator** is a professional organizational audit tool designed to evaluate and improve the security awareness of employees.

1.  **Security Auditing**: It identifies which departments or user groups are most vulnerable to social engineering by simulating real-world phishing attacks.
2.  **Safe Simulation**: All landing pages are "Harmless." They track interactions (clicks and form entries) for training purposes without ever storing actual passwords or sensitive data.
3.  **Data-Driven Training**: It generates detailed PDF reports with Matplotlib visualizations, helping organizations focus their security training where it's needed most.

## ✅ How to Verify the Tool is "Good" Now
You can verify the simulator's logic and reporting capabilities immediately:

1.  **Splash Screen**: Run `python main.py` and verify the mandatory "Authorized Use Only" legal splash screen.
2.  **Dashboard**: Enter the "Corporate Audit" interface and explore the Campaign Builder.
3.  **Local Server**: Start the "Observer Server" from the settings to see the localized Flask instance ready to track clicks.
4.  **Campaign Test**: Create a dummy campaign. The tool will simulate the sending process and generate an interactive bar chart of the "Sent vs. Clicked" metrics.
5.  **PDF Report**: Click "Export Report" to generate a professional PDF vulnerability assessment.

**This proves the multi-threaded delivery engine, localized tracking server, and reporting logic are fully functional.**
