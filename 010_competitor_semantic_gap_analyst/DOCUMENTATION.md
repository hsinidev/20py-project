## Overview
The Competitor Semantic Gap Analyst is an executive-level intelligence tool designed to identify and bridge "semantic gaps" in AI search engine results. It analyzes how modern LLMs (Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity) perceive a brand relative to its top competitors for high-value industry keywords.

## Technical Architecture
1. **Semantic Matrix Engine**: Built on **Pandas**, this engine processes keyword authority scores across multiple entities. It isolates "Topical Gaps"—areas where competitors are cited as authoritative while the target brand is missing or underrepresented.
2. **Executive Dashboard**: A **Tkinter** GUI using the **Tksheet** library for high-density data management and **Plotly** for browser-embedded interactive radar charts.
3. **Roadmap Generator**: Uses heuristic NLP to generate **GEO-optimized** content briefs. These briefs provide a direct "Roadmap to Dominance" by suggesting content injections that trigger RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems to favor the user's brand.

## Key Features
- **4-Way Competitive Matrix**: Compare your brand against 3 competitors simultaneously.
- **Interactive Radar Charts**: Visual representation of semantic coverage and topical deficits.
- **Multi-Sheet Executive Excel**: Professional data exports with conditional formatting.
- **Markdown-It Content Briefs**: Strategic roadmap for content teams to fill identified gaps.
