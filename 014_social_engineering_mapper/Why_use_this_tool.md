# Why Use the Social Engineering Vulnerability Mapper?

Social engineering is responsible for over 90% of successful enterprise data breaches. This tool provides a proactive defense by mapping your organization's human attack surface.

### 1. Identify "Over-Sharers"
The AI engine identifies employees whose public profiles leak sensitive technical details (e.g., "Maintained our legacy Oracle 11g server") which attackers use to craft highly targeted spear-phishing campaigns.

### 2. Strategic Visualizations
Traditional spreadsheets are difficult to parse for non-technical stakeholders. The **Live Risk Matrix** provides an instant visual heat-map, showing executives exactly which departments are most at risk.

### 3. Automated PII Auditing
Manually checking hundreds of profiles for PII (Phone numbers, personal emails, physical locations) is impossible. Our **Playwright Cluster** automates this, doing weeks of manual work in minutes.

### 4. Enterprise-Grade Reporting
The generated HTML reports are designed for the C-Suite. They don't just show problems; they provide **Mitigation Steps** to reduce risk and improve organizational security posture.

### 5. High Performance
By using **Asynchronous Task-Queue Architecture (ATQA)** and multiprocessing, the system remains lightning-fast even when auditing large multinational corporations.
